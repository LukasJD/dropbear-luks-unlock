# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This is a beginner-facing guide. Many people reading it are new to Linux, encryption, or homelabbing in general, and asking a "basic" question here took some courage. We want that to always feel safe to do.

## Expected behavior

- Be patient with questions that seem obvious to you — everyone starts somewhere
- Give constructive, specific feedback ("this step needs X because Y" rather than "this is wrong")
- Assume good faith
- Respect that people are running this on their own personal equipment and have their own risk tolerance and constraints

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or belittling comments directed at any contributor or question-asker
- Gatekeeping ("if you don't know this already you shouldn't be doing homelab stuff")
- Off-topic political or inflammatory content in issues/PRs

## Scope

This applies to all repository spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other official community channels linked from this repo.

## Enforcement

Violations may result in comment removal, a warning, or — for repeated or severe issues — a block from the repository, at the maintainer's discretion.

## Reporting

If something in this repo's spaces makes you uncomfortable, open an issue if you're comfortable doing so publicly, or reach out to the maintainer directly if not (add your preferred contact method here before publishing).

This is a lightweight, project-specific code of conduct. For a more comprehensive standard template, see the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), which many open-source projects adopt directly.
